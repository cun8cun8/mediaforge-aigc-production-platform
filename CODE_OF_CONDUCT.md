# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We expect collaboration to be respectful, specific and focused on improving the work. Product, security, architecture and review disagreements are normal; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

When reviewing work:

- Discuss evidence, behavior, risk and alternatives rather than making assumptions about intent.
- Keep feedback actionable and proportionate to the change.
- Do not share customer materials, credentials, private prompts or unreleased business information outside approved channels.
- Escalate security, rights or tenant-isolation concerns privately instead of debating sensitive details in public threads.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, revoke access or take other proportionate steps when this standard is violated. Report a concern privately to the repository owner through GitHub. For security matters, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
